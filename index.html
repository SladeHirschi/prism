HOW TO PLAY

  • WEAR ITS COLOUR TO TAKE IT. An orb can only be picked up while you are the same colour as it.
  • WEAR ITS COLOUR TO SURVIVE IT. Waves and shapes kill you — unless you match them. Everything warns you before it arrives.
  • DASH GOES THROUGH ANYTHING. Any colour, no damage. The dotted ring means it is ready.
  • DON'T BE SLOW. Each orb has a timer. Let one run out and the run is over. 15 orbs to clear.
